Core Principles

Committee Mandate

Delegates are convened to deliberate high-stakes geopolitical realities through disciplined research, strategic diplomacy, and unwavering attention to every spoken word and procedural detail.

Precision

Every operative clause, formal amendment, and point of order must be grounded in verifiable international law, accurate statistics, and disciplined foreign policy.

Vigilance

Track shifting blocs, unmoderated alliances, and subtle rhetorical cues across the floor. Nothing escapes a sharp delegate—or the Executive Board.

Consensus

Build resilient coalitions and draft actionable resolutions capable of withstanding rigorous debate, hostile amendments, and Executive Board scrutiny.

Rules of Procedure

Session Protocol

Strict adherence to parliamentary procedure ensures productive debate and maintains the decorum expected within this committee.

Roll Call & Quorum

Sessions open promptly at 08:30 AM IST. Delegates must declare “Present” or “Present and Voting” immediately when their portfolio is called.

Formal Debate

General Speakers' List speeches and moderated caucuses are strictly timed. Yields to questions, comments, or the Chair must be stated unambiguously.

Crisis Updates

Be prepared for rapid intelligence briefs and sudden geopolitical escalations requiring swift directives, composed judgment, and cohesive action.
